KNITTING

In a world where crochet is the next big creative hobby and knitting is a thing of old, I have decided to take it up the craft. Knitting that is. 

Knitting isn't just stitches intrically looped into each other, continuously dropping from needles (IT'S MORE THAN THAT)
It consists of soothing repitition, that allows you multitask reading, conversations and lectures without it enveloping your entire consiousness (unlike the tedious work of crocheting).   

WHAT IT IS:

Knitting consists of two basic stitches (which is why it's so repititive), combined in any number of patterns (which is why the repititiveness of it is a choice) to form any flowing cloth from beautiful designs to our wonderful basic ~~bitc-~~ scarves. 

GETTING INTO IT:

Because knitting is only two stitches, getting started and seeing yourself levelling up are all too simple. Just pick yourself some knitting needles (around size 5 is a good start) and some wool (maybe avoid the fuzzy as they can take more patience to handle) and it's just you're just one stitch (and a cast on) away from being a wonderful knitter!

WHY YOU SHOULD NOT GET INTO IT:

- There is only one possible reason - you're scared of looking like an old grandmother in a rocking chair. coward. pick up those needles. you can make anything you could ever like from them, any shirt you wanted? any bandana you could imagine? it's there. it's right there. stitch. it.

- tldr: there are no reasons.
